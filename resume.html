How I operate

Four things that are always
true about how I work.

01

Start with the person, not the product

Every product I've built started with someone stuck or underserved. I stay in the discomfort until I understand it fully.

02

Ship to learn, not to launch

I don't design in theory. I build to the smallest unit of truth and let real users tell me what's wrong.

03

AI as a tool, not a trick

I use AI the way a surgeon uses a scalpel. Precisely, in service of the work. Not to generate noise.

04

Zero to one is the whole job

I operate without a playbook. Fintech, faith-tech, edtech, consumer. Always building toward the first real signal.
