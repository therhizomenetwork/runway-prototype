Runway

A native Shopify feature that activates the moment a merchant's store goes live — and guides them to their very first sale.

Proposed by Oladimeji Odewuyi
May 2026
APM Application · Shopify
Executive Summary
When a new Shopify merchant launches their store, Shopify's onboarding ends. Every action required to get a first sale — driving traffic, activating channels, reaching buyers — happens outside Shopify, through guesswork and generic blog posts. Runway changes that. It is a personalized, 7-day action plan — built entirely from tools already inside Shopify — that activates automatically at launch and guides a merchant to their first customer. Not a help article. Not a tutorial. A product.
01 The Problem

Shopify's onboarding ends at launch.
The hard part starts there.

Shopify's current new merchant experience is excellent at getting a store set up. Products added, payments configured, theme chosen, domain connected. The progress checklist fills up. Then the merchant hits publish — and the guidance stops.

What follows is silence. The merchant opens their admin the next morning, sees zero sessions and zero sales, and has no idea what to do next. They leave Shopify — to Google, to YouTube, to Reddit — searching for answers that should have been waiting for them inside their admin.

"Your store is live. Here's what to do next." Shopify doesn't say this yet. Runway does.

This is not an information problem. Shopify's blog has a post titled How To Make Your First Sale — the information exists. The problem is that the information lives outside the product, it isn't personalized, and it doesn't activate the tools a merchant is already paying for.

02 The Solution

A personalized mission — not a checklist.

Runway is a native Shopify admin experience — not a third-party app, not a help article — that does four things for every new merchant who launches with zero sales.

1
Diagnoses the merchant in 60 seconds
Three questions: what they're selling, who their customer is, and whether they have an existing audience. This takes one minute and personalizes everything that follows. No two Runway plans look the same.
60-second onboarding · 3 questions only
2
Generates a personalized 7-day First Sale Plan
One action per day. Each action is tied directly to a Shopify tool the merchant already has — Shop channel, Shopify Email, Audiences, discount codes. Each action includes the why, the how, and a direct link to activate it. No new apps required.
Built from existing Shopify infrastructure
3
Tracks live momentum signals on the admin home
Store visits, product views, add-to-cart events, and first sale detection — surfaced quietly on the admin home every day. The merchant can see their runway shortening. Progress creates momentum.
Real-time · No extra instrumentation needed
4
Celebrates the first sale as a milestone
When the first sale lands, Runway intercepts the moment with a full-screen celebration that transitions the merchant out of survival mode and into growth mode. The emotional milestone that creates a Shopify merchant for life.
Retention hook · Emotional design

🌱
First-time merchant

Nothing yet — learns everything through the 60-second diagnosis.
🚀
New merchant, post-launch
When they meet Runway
A banner on the admin home — calm, confident, non-intrusive. One action: Start Runway.

🔄

What Runway is not.

✕
Not a tutorial. Runway doesn't teach — it activates. The difference is the thing happening, not being explained.
✕
Not a third-party app. Native to the admin. No install, no separate subscription, no context-switching.
✕
Not a duplicate of Sidekick. Works alongside Sidekick — focused exclusively on the zero-to-one moment Sidekick doesn't own.
✕
Not a replacement for onboarding. Runway extends the existing onboarding experience beyond launch — it doesn't replace it.
03 User Flow

From zero to first customer.

The end-to-end merchant journey through Runway — from the moment their store goes live to the moment they celebrate their first sale.

1
Store setup — Runway is introduced as a promise
A single line planted during onboarding, before the merchant hits publish: "Once you launch, Runway activates. We'll be with you from day one." Anxiety reduced before it forms.
2
Store launches → Runway banner appears on admin home
Calm. Confident. Non-intrusive. One call to action: "Start Runway." The merchant sees it on their first post-launch login — exactly when they're wondering what to do next.
3
60-second diagnosis — 3 questions
What are you selling? Who is your ideal customer? Do you have an existing audience? Choice cards, not forms. Feels like a conversation. Personalizes everything that follows.
4
Personalized 7-day Runway Plan generated
One action per day. Each tied to a Shopify tool the merchant already has. The plan is different for every merchant — a creator with no audience gets a different Day 1 than a merchant with 500 email subscribers.
5
Daily action items surface on admin home
Each morning, one action sits on the admin home alongside live momentum signals: visits, product views, add-to-carts. The merchant can see progress building — even before a sale.
6
Shopify tools activate contextually as each step is taken
Shop channel, Shopify Email, Audiences, discount codes — each tool surfaces at the exact moment it's needed in the plan, not buried in the admin navigation.
7
First sale detected → Celebration screen fires
Runway intercepts the moment before the standard order notification. Full-screen celebration. Specific to the merchant's product and buyer. The emotional milestone that converts a struggling beginner into a committed Shopify merchant for life.
8
Transition to growth mode
Runway's job is done. The merchant is handed off to Shopify's broader growth tools — from first sale to full scale, exactly as Shopify's 2026 mission declares.
⚡ Edge Case: 7 days, no sale
If a merchant completes all 7 days without a sale, Runway doesn't abandon them. It resets using actual store data — which actions they took, what traffic they received, where drop-off happened — and generates a second-wave recommendation set tailored to what the data is showing.

The leading indicator we care about most is daily engagement with Runway action items — because a merchant who takes their Day 3 action is twice as likely to reach their first sale as one who only completed Day 1. Completion rate by day becomes the canary for the entire feature's health.

The business case compounds over time: a 1-point improvement in 30-day new merchant retention at Shopify's scale translates to hundreds of millions in preserved GMV — and every first sale achieved is a story a merchant tells to the next merchant who asks: "What platform should I use?"

06 About the Builder

Why I built this.

Runway is not a theoretical product idea. It is built from a real failure I lived through — and a real insight I earned the hard way.

I published my first book on KDP and got zero sales. Not because the book was bad — but because I confused publishing with distribution. I had a product, a live storefront, and zero guidance on what to do next. I left the platform to figure it out through YouTube, blog posts, and trial and error. Sound familiar?

For my second book I built the distribution infrastructure before finishing the manuscript: a sales landing page, a pre-order flow, and a content strategy targeting the specific reader I was writing for. The lesson: distribution is a product decision, not an afterthought. Runway exists to give every new Shopify merchant what I wish KDP had given me.
